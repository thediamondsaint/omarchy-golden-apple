# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

- Test on a real Omarchy 4 install: `./install.sh`, then `hyprctl configerrors` must be empty.
- Keep the shell surfaces translucent and gold-tinted; keep the terminal `alpha` inside `[colors-dark]`.
- Do not use `ignore_alpha` on the layer blur rule; it disables the blur on those surfaces.
- New wallpapers: JPEG, under ~2 MB, at least 2560×1600.
- Screenshots go in `docs/` and must not show personal windows or data.
