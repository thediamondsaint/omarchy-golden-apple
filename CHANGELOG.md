# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Golden Apple (dark) and Golden Apple Light: champagne-gold palettes with warm ANSI colours.
- Gold-tinted glass on the bar, menus, launcher, popups and notifications; solid gold selection pill.
- Terminal transparency through foot's `alpha`, with fully opaque text.
- 14px continuous corners, hairline gold gradient borders, soft shadow, Home Screen-style motion.
- A gold lion wallpaper plus four generated gradients per variant.
- Bar icons follow the theme: the installer turns off the bar's wallpaper-sampling transparent mode.
- `tools/add-wallpaper.sh` and `tools/make-wallpapers.sh`.
- `install.sh` (both variants, remembers your previous theme, replaces a git-cloned copy) and `uninstall.sh`.
